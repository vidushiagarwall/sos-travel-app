Brand core

Who HerWay is

Mission

Help every woman travel solo with the people she trusts one tap away.

Personality in 5 words

CalmWarmPracticalPlayfulHonest

Value 1

Calm before clever

In a scary moment, the next step is the only thing on screen. Big buttons, few words, no clutter.

Value 2

Every fact has a source

A wrong number can hurt someone. Every number shows where it came from and when we last checked it.

Value 3

Family is part of the trip

Mum and Dad worry at home. HerWay gives them a plan too, so she can enjoy the trip.

Voice

How HerWay talks

Like an older cousin who has done this trip already. Short sentences, plain words, "you" and "we". Warm, never cutesy. In an emergency, calm and direct.

Do"Save your hotel address offline before you land."
Don't"It is strongly advised that users pre-download accommodation details."
Do"Your circle will get your location."
Don't"Location payload dispatched to trusted contact array."
Do"Most missed calls are a dead battery. Here's what to do if it's longer."
Don't"Every year thousands of women go missing abroad."
Do"Call 112 first. Then tell your circle."
Don't"Stay safe, queen! 💅✨"
Do"We couldn't confirm this number yet, so we haven't listed it."
Don'tGuessing a number, or hiding that you didn't find one.

SOS

Red only ever means SOS

If something is red, tapping it sends for help. Errors, delete buttons, warnings and hospital numbers are never red. Errors use ink text on a soft grey box, delete uses a plain ghost button, and hospital numbers use lavender ink.

Fix needed: the site sets --hospital: #e05260, the same red as SOS. Change it to #6E5BA8.

:root {
  --bg: #f6f8f8;
  --text: #1f2a2e;
  --muted: #5b6b70;
  --accent: #2e8b8b;        /* logo, icons, big headings */
  --accent-dark: #216c6c;   /* buttons, links, small teal text */
  --accent-soft: #e0efef;
  --lavender: #9a8cc2;      /* decoration only */
  --lavender-ink: #6e5ba8;  /* NEW: handwriting, lavender text */
  --lavender-soft: #ece8f5;
  --danger: #e05260;        /* SOS only */
  --police: #2e6fe0;
  --hospital: #6e5ba8;      /* was red, red is SOS only */
}

Look & feel

Postcards, pins and soft circles

Illustration

Flat shapes, no outlines. Teal and lavender only, on soft circles. Dotted flight paths join things up. No faces, no fear: never dark streets or shadowy figures.

Photos

Real Indian women on real trips, in daylight: a station, a café, a street market. Shot from behind or the side, candid, not posed. Soft, slightly warm colours. Never stock photos of women looking scared.

Icons

Line icons, 24px, 2px stroke, round ends, in dark teal. The free Lucide set matches this exactly. Never mix in filled or emoji icons.

Corners & shadows

Inputs 10px, cards 14px, big cards and postcards 22px, buttons and chips fully round. One soft shadow (--shadow-sm) on cards, --shadow-md only on hover or a postcard. No hard borders and shadows together.
