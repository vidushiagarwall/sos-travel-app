# Must haves (Milestone 1 wishlist)

These are shown on the site in the "What's coming" section (src/components/WhatsComing.tsx).

1. A country safety guide: type where you're travelling and get one page for that country, with emergency numbers, women's helplines, the nearest Indian embassy, scams and safety tips.
2. Emergency numbers for every country: tap to call, available offline.
3. SOS message: one tap sends your live location to your contacts.
4. Offline mode with critical emergency phrases and maps, for areas without reliable data or wifi.
5. A log in portal, so that each person has an account to record their data.
6. Each woman using the website will have her own account, where she can save her emergency contact, and notify them in case of any unsafe situations.
7. A check-in feature that alerts an emergency contact if you don't check in within a set time.
8. A guide for the family back home: HerWay's trip share can include a "what to do if you can't reach her" page with local emergency numbers, embassy contact and hotel details.
9. The "first hour after landing" kit. Airport to hotel safely: official taxi counters, which ride apps actually work there, SIM tips, what to save offline.
10. "Show this screen" cards for women-specific situations. Big, offline phrase cards in the local language, like "I am being followed", "I need a female police officer", or "Take me to the nearest police station".
11. Location-based cultural context, like local dress norms or common scams, surfaced automatically based on GPS. Also makes users aware of tourist traps.
12. A map showing safe places near the user's current location: nearest hospital, police station and pharmacy.
13. A composite safety score for each location, built by combining multiple data sources (advisories, crime data where available, lighting/infrastructure data) into one number, with a clear methodology detailing how each factor is weighted.
14. A personalised risk profile, where a user's inputs (time of day, budget level, solo vs. with a guide) adjust the score they see.
